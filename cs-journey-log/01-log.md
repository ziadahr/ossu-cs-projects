## Building the Habit

This year, I turned 26. I originally started around December 2025, but I stopped at Lecture 5 of Intro to CS (MIT Python 6.0001).

In April, I came back with a new mindset. I don't know exactly what changed, but I committed to studying—even if it was just 10 minutes a day. If I missed a few days, I didn't quit; I simply picked up where I left off.

Day by day, the momentum built until I was naturally studying 1–2 hours a day. Once I finished Intro to CS and all the problem sets, I was ready for the real CS Core.

## The August Sprint

I started *Systematic Program Design (SPD)* lightly in late July, but in August, everything clicked. I started sleeping early and waking up at 7 AM (sometimes 8 or 9 if I needed the extra rest). I was locked in, studying 3–7 hours every day.

Lately, moving into September, I've felt my focus drop slightly. I know exactly why: the concepts have become incredibly difficult. Topics like **Generative Recursion**, **Search**, **BSTs**, and **Arbitrary-Arity Trees** consume a massive amount of mental energy.

> **💡 Note to self:** Stop losing an entire day to one problem. Moving forward, I will limit my "struggle phase" to 20–40 minutes. If I am totally stuck, I'll check a hint and try again rather than burning myself out.
